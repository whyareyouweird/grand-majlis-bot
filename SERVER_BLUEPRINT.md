# 🕊️ Khafos Islamic Community & Educational Majlis
## Complete Discord Server Blueprint & Architecture

> **Aesthetic Theme:** Minimalist White / Pearl / Marble / Ivory  
> **Target Audience:** Fans of Khafos, Students of Knowledge, Muslims seeking authentic educational content & community brotherhood/sisterhood.

---

### 🎨 Aesthetic Palette & Fonts
Channels and categories use clean Unicode formatting (serif small caps & elegant symbols) to achieve a calm, clean, luminous white appearance.

* **Primary Symbols:** `✧` `✦` `・` `┊` `╰` `╭─` `🕊️` `📜` `📖` `🤍` `🥛` `🕋` `🌙` `🎙️`
* **Category Bracket Format:** `╭─・[EMOJI] ∙ [CATEGORY NAME]`
* **Channel Formats:**
  * Informational / Read-Only: `┊・[EMOJI]・[name]`
  * Educational: `┊・[EMOJI]・[name]`
  * Last channel of category: `╰・[EMOJI]・[name]`
