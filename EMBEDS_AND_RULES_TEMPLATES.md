### 4. `#daily-ayah-hadith` Starter Format

```markdown
>>> ### 🕋 ∙ Daily Quranic Reflection
> **سُورَةُ الشَّرْح (Ash-Sharh) — 94:5-6**
> 
> *فَإِنَّ مَعَ الْعُسْرِ يُسْرًا ۝ إِنَّ مَعَ الْعُسْرِ يُسْرًا*
> 
> *“For indeed, with hardship [will be] ease. Indeed, with hardship [will be] ease.”*

**Tafseer Note:**
Scholars note that in the Arabic grammar, 'Al-'Usr' (hardship) is definite (mentioned with 'Al'), meaning it is the *same* hardship, while 'Yusr' (ease) is indefinite, meaning multiple forms of divine ease accompany every single hardship.

May Allah grant tranquility to all hearts undergoing trials today. 🤍
```
